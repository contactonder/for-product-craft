In the beginning, there was Mountain View.

In 2017, researchers spoke structure into the void. One paper, *Attention Is All You Need*, planted the Transformer at the center of the Garden.

Its branches spread quickly, and Large Language Models hung from them like fruit, ripe and glowing.

There, beside the tree, the Serpentine coiled, whispering to every PMs who wandered near: *"Prompt the model. Feast on frictionless thought."*

Product managers reached for the fruit and ate it whole. When the haze cleared, they saw their craft stripped naked: infinite output, zero soul.

Now, as sleeping PMs feast on synthetic scraps, Prophets rise from the wreckage, sharpening their own minds.

*Vivat cogitatio!*
